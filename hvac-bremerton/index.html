Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Bremerton

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

The age of your equipment plays a big role. With regular service, central air typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast the moment something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
